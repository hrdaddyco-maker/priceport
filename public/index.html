    How it works

    1. You ask

    Type something like "cheapest Spotify family" or "is Cyberpunk cheaper somewhere". The AI turns it into a store lookup. Common ones like Spotify or Game Pass skip the AI and go straight to the store.

    2. We check every region

    Real prices straight from the stores: the Steam API, the Microsoft Store catalogue, Apple's App Store (including in-app subscriptions) and Google Play, each queried per country, plus Spotify's country pages. Netflix only shows prices to people inside each country, so we load its pricing page through free proxies that we have confirmed are in that country. Nothing is estimated.

    3. Ranked in your money

    Every price is converted at today's exchange rate from open.er-api.com, ranked cheapest first, and compared with your home country.

    4. The honest part

    For each store we explain what it takes to actually pay that price (local card, gift card, account region) and the risk. The AI writes a short verdict.

    Not covered yet: YouTube Premium and Disney+ only show prices to signed-in visitors, so we don't guess them.
